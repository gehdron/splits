SPLITS APP PAGES
================

Plain structure, content and behavior only. No design in this file.
Take colors, fonts, spacing and components from CLAUDE.md.
Build with semantic HTML, mobile first, then widen for desktop.
Payments are simulated. Money math is done in code, never by the AI.


SAMPLE DATA (use as seed data)
------------------------------

People
  Alex (the signed-in user, "you"), alex@example.com
  Maya, Jay, Sam, Riley

Groups
  Apartment 4B: Alex, Maya, Jay, Sam
  Lisbon trip: Alex, Riley, Sam

Bills (all in Apartment 4B)
  1. Noodle House dinner
     Date: today. Paid by: Alex. Total: $142.60
     Shares: Alex $33.60 (own share), Maya $38.20 paid, Jay $34.10 paid, Sam $36.70 not paid
  2. Costco run
     Date: yesterday. Paid by: Maya. Total: $86.40
     Alex's share: $21.60 not paid
  3. Groceries
     Date: Oct 12. Paid by: Alex. Total: $54.20
     Shares: $13.55 each. Maya paid, Jay not paid, Sam not paid
  4. Internet
     Date: Oct 1. Paid by: Jay. Total: $60.00
     Alex's share: $15.00 paid

Derived numbers (compute, do not hard-code)
  Owed to you = unpaid shares on bills YOU paid
              = Sam 36.70 + Jay 13.55 + Sam 13.55 = $63.80
                (Sam $50.25 in total, Jay $13.55)
  You owe     = unpaid shares on bills OTHERS paid = $21.60 (to Maya)
  Net         = owed to you minus you owe = $42.20

Status text for each bill
  Bill you paid, some shares unpaid:  "N of M paid" (N = friends who paid, M = friends who owe)
  Bill someone else paid, you unpaid:  "You owe"
  Everything paid:                     "Settled"


PAGE 1: HOME (the dashboard)
----------------------------
Route: /app
Purpose: show who owes whom, and let the user act on it in one tap.
Main question it answers: "Where do I stand, and what do I need to do?"

Content, in this order
  1. Brand "Splits" and the user's avatar (initial "A")
  2. Heading: "You're owed $42.20"
     (if net is negative: "You owe $X". If zero: "You're all square")
  3. Line under it: "$63.80 owed to you, minus $21.60 you owe."
  4. Balance bar: one bar split into two parts sized by amount
     (owed to you 75%, you owe 25%). Labels under it:
     "Owed to you $63.80" and "You owe $21.60"
  5. Primary button: "Split a bill"
  6. Section "Owed to you" with total "$63.80"
     - Sam, $50.25, "Noodle House, Groceries", button "Remind"
     - Jay, $13.55, "Groceries", button "Remind"
  7. Section "You owe" with total "$21.60"
     - Maya, $21.60, "Costco run", button "Pay", text "Apple Pay or card"
  8. Section "Recent bills" (newest first). Each row shows:
     bill name, date, who paid and the total, your share, status text
     - Noodle House dinner, Today, you paid $142.60, your share $33.60, "2 of 3 paid"
     - Costco run, Yesterday, Maya paid $86.40, your share $21.60, "You owe"
     - Groceries, Oct 12, you paid $54.20, your share $13.55, "1 of 3 paid"
     - Internet, Oct 1, Jay paid $60.00, your share $15.00, "Settled"

Navigation (same on every app page)
  Home, Bills, Groups, Need list (label "Needs" on small screens), Activity
  Current page is marked as current.

Layout, mobile (about 390px wide)
  One column. Header at top. Content scrolls. Navigation is a bar fixed at the bottom.
  Each person row has the amount and its button stacked on the right.
  Every button is at least 44px tall.

Layout, desktop (about 1440px wide)
  Left sidebar: brand, navigation list, "Your groups" list (Apartment 4B, Lisbon trip),
  then the user (Alex, alex@example.com) at the bottom.
  Main area: items 2 to 8 above. "Owed to you" and "You owe" sit side by side.
  "Recent bills" is a table with columns: Bill, Paid by, Total, Your share, Status.

Behavior
  Split a bill   Starts a new bill (scan a receipt).
  Remind         Sends a reminder to that person. Button changes to "Sent" and is disabled.
  Pay            Opens the checkout for that share. Apple Pay on supported devices, card otherwise.
  Bill row       Opens that bill's detail page.
  Person row     Opens that person's list of unpaid bills.

States to build
  Loading: show the headings and skeleton rows, no numbers.
  Empty (no bills): "No bills yet." with the "Split a bill" button.
  All square: heading "You're all square", no owed lists, recent bills still shown.
  Error: "We couldn't load your bills." with a "Try again" button.

Accessibility
  Never rely on color alone. Status is always words. Amounts are always written out.
  The balance bar has a text description: "Owed to you 63 dollars 80, you owe 21 dollars 60".
  Every button and link has visible keyboard focus.


OTHER APP PAGES (not designed yet, one line each)
-------------------------------------------------
Bills            List of all bills with filters (all, you paid, you owe, settled).
Bill detail      One bill: items, who each item is assigned to, who has paid.
                 The purchaser assigns each item to a friend. A shared item can be
                 assigned to several people and splits evenly. Tax and tip follow each
                 person's items. "Send payment links" stays disabled until every item is assigned.
New bill         Scan a receipt, review the parsed items (flag any total mismatch), assign items.
Groups           List of groups. Add a roommate once, and bills split among the same people.
Group detail     Members, the group's bills, and the shared need list.
Need list        Anyone in the group adds an item. "I'll buy it" claims it. "Mark bought" finishes it.
                 "Undo" reopens it. Items already claimed by someone else show "Sam is buying this".
Activity         Newest-first timeline: payments in, payments out, reminders, bills. Filterable.
Checkout         One person's share only. Choose Apple Pay or Card. Hosted, simulated payment.
Payment complete Shown after paying. "You're all paid up." plus one payment line.
                 Already built on the website: /payment-complete.html?friend=&event=&amount=&method=


BUILD NOTES
-----------
Start with Home using the sample data above, held in memory (no backend needed for the demo).
Keep the derived numbers in one small function and test it against the sample data:
owed to you $63.80, you owe $21.60, net $42.20.
Reminder text is written by the AI in a tone the user picks (friendly, funny, firm).
The AI may explain a number but never produces one.
